# AptiQuiz Brand

![AptiQuiz](Apti-App/apps/web/public/brand/aptiquiz-logo-readme.png)

## Logo

The AptiQuiz identity is an orange Q/check symbol paired with a dark ink wordmark. The mark connects aptitude questions, correct answers, and progress.

Use the shared assets in `Apti-App/apps/web/public/brand/`: `aptiquiz-logo.png` for light interfaces, `aptiquiz-logo-readme.png` for documentation on either light or dark themes, and `aptiquiz-icon.png` for browser icons and compact identity. The documentation edition has an ivory background to keep its dark wordmark legible.

Keep the original proportions and clear space; do not stretch, recolor, or add effects. Display the full lockup at least 144px wide. Give it one accessible name, “AptiQuiz”; use an empty image alt when a containing link already supplies that name. These PNGs are raster assets; editable vector sources are not available. See [logo provenance](design-mocks/aptiquiz-logo/README.md) for generation prompts and the original asset.

The older leaf mark and existing generated screen PNGs are historical references. Current interfaces and prototypes use the Q/check identity.

## Positioning

AptiQuiz turns solitary placement aptitude practice into a fair live competition. It is built for students who need to improve speed under pressure and colleges that want practice events students actually repeat.

## Voice

Direct, energetic, precise, and encouraging—not childish, corporate, or exam-anxious.

Sounds right: “Race the clock. Climb the room.”

Sounds wrong: “Leverage our revolutionary gamified assessment ecosystem.”

Use short active sentences during a live match. Errors explain what happened and what the Participant can do next without blame.

## Naming

- Product name: **AptiQuiz**.
- Capitalize the domain terms defined in [`CONTEXT.md`](CONTEXT.md) when referring to a product concept in technical documents.
- User-facing controls use familiar sentence case: “Join a room”, “Start match”, “Submit answer”.
- Never imply that speed alone equals ability; accuracy and improvement remain visible.

## Non-Goals

- Do not mimic a childish trivia game.
- Do not glorify placement rejection or shame low-ranking Participants.
- Do not claim perfect fairness, cheating prevention, or capacity without test evidence.
- Do not market deferred Redis or Kafka infrastructure as a user benefit.
