AptiQuiz / interaction studies

Twelve ways to make a call to action feel considered.

Hover, focus, click, and scroll through the studies. Each works without a mouse and respects reduced-motion preferences.

01 / Magnetic pull

Come a little closer.

A small parallax pull rewards precision on desktop pointers.

02 / Sliding text

A fresh line, in motion.

The label rolls into its next state one character at a time.

03 / Liquid fill

A precise splash.

An orange circle starts at the pointer and takes over the button.

04 / Orbiting border

Energy at the edge.

A fine conic ring keeps the dark action surface alive.

05 / Shine sweep

A little lightness.

The hero version also plays once every five seconds.

06 / Spotlight follow

Move the light.

A soft radial highlight follows the cursor inside the action.

07 / Spring press

Clear, tactile feedback.

Lift on intent; compress on press.

08 / Arrow chase

Keep moving forward.

The icon departs and returns to give the label a nudge forward.

09 / Idle glow

An invitation, quietly.

Reserve this slow pulse for the one primary CTA on a page.

10 / Success morph

A satisfying confirmation.

Press to see the label settle into a spinner and an earned check.

11 / Scroll reveal

Arrive with purpose.

The CTA opens from below and gives one restrained landing ping.

12 / Click ripple

Make contact visible.

A blurred brand ripple begins exactly where the button is pressed.
